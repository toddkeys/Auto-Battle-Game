How to play

  1. Buy heroes in the shop. They wait on the bench (the wooden deck).
  2. Drag a hero onto your half of the field (or click it, then a cell). Drop it on another hero to swap; drop it on the shop to sell.
  3. 3 copies merge into ★★ automatically (3 × ★★ = ★★★).
  4. Bonuses: 2 or 3 different heroes of the same class or origin unlock a bonus (copies count once).
  5. Press Start battle — heroes fight on their own. A full blue mana bar casts their skill.
  6. Summoners call allies (wisps, wolves, a stone guardian, a star drake) that fight until the battle ends.
  7. Every 5th round is a monster round; from round 15 a boss joins. Bosses alternate two skills, enrage at half HP and always drop a rare item.
  8. Items: drag onto a hero (1 each). Rare items shimmer. 📜 Unbinding Scroll (rare drop) takes a hero's item off.
  9. Statuses: 💫 Stun 1s · 🤐 Silence 2s · 🌫️ Blind 2s · 🐌 Slow · ☠️ Poison · 🧊 Frozen — ice skills may freeze; any direct hit shatters it. Bosses can't be frozen.
  10. Shop odds: 2💰 heroes from round 4, 3💰 from round 8, 4💰 from round 12. "Lock" keeps the shop for next round.
  11. Field slots: start with 3, buy more up to 9 (6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36 gold).
  12. Gold: 5 per round + 1 interest per 10 saved (max 5). Losing costs HP; at 0 the run ends.
Sprite settings

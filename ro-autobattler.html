How to play
  1. Buy units from the shop. They go to the bench (bottom row).
  2. Drag a unit onto a cell in the lower half of the field (or click it, then click a cell). Drop it on another unit to swap.
  3. Bonuses: every unit has a class and an origin. 2 or 3 different units of the same class or origin unlock a bonus (copies count once).
  4. "Lock" keeps the current shop for next round instead of rerolling it.
  5. Press "Start battle" and the units fight on their own.
  6. Units fill their blue mana bar by attacking and taking hits. When it is full they cast their skill.
  7. Every 5th round is a monster round (from round 15 a boss joins). Win it to get items; 📜 Unbinding Scroll takes a unit's items off.
  8. Enemies get stronger every round, build around class/origin bonuses and carry items later on. Bosses enrage at half HP.
  9. Status effects: 💫 Stun 1s (can't act), 🤐 Silence 2s (can't cast skills), 🌫️ Blind 2s (50% of attacks miss), 🐌 Slow (-40% move and attack speed), ☠️ Poison (damage over time). The unit glows in the status colour and a badge above it shows the time left. Holy healers cleanse them.
  10. Assassins leap behind the enemy front line when a battle starts — keep your back line protected.
  11. Drag a unit onto the shop to sell it.
  12. Shop odds: 1💰 units only at first, 2💰 from round 4, 3💰 from round 8, and the strongest 4💰 units from round 12.
  13. Phone: press and hold a shop unit to see its stats; swipe on empty grass to scroll.
  14. 3 copies of the same unit merge into ★★ automatically (3 × ★★ = ★★★).
  15. You start with 3 field slots. Press "+1 slot" to buy more (up to 9); each slot costs more than the last (6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36 gold).
  16. Each round gives 5 gold + 1 interest per 10 gold saved (max 5).
  17. Reach 0 HP and the game is over.

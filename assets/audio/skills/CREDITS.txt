Hero and boss skill signature remixes

All .ogg files in this directory are remixes of the shipped SFX named in
profiles.json (the sources field). Remixes: pitch shifts, equalization,
layering, timing, fades and two-pass loudness matching by this project's
contributors. Rebuild with tools/build_signature_audio.py.

Source authors and packs:
- p0ss, Spell Sounds Starter Pack, CC BY-SA 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/spell-sounds-starter-pack
- artisticdude, RPG Sound Pack, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
- rubberduck, 80 CC0 RPG SFX / 25 CC0 bang and firework SFX, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx
  https://opengameart.org/content/25-cc0-bang-firework-sfx

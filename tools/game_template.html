How to play
  1. Buy heroes in the shop. They wait on the bench (the wooden deck).
  2. Drag a hero to move it (drop on another hero to swap). Click selects; click empty ground to cancel. Right-click a hero or drop it on the shop to sell.
  3. 3 copies merge into ★★ automatically (3 × ★★ = ★★★).
  4. Bonuses: 2 or 3 different heroes of the same class or origin unlock a bonus (copies count once).
  5. Press Start battle — heroes fight on their own. A full blue mana bar casts their skill.
  6. Crusaders link to the nearest back-line ally (gold tether) and take part of its damage.
  7. Click a fighter during battle to see its live stats and skill.
  8. Selling returns half the gold — or all of it if you bought the hero this round.
  9. Summoners call allies (wisps, wolves, a stone guardian, a star drake) that fight until the battle ends.
  10. Every 5th round is a monster round; from round 15 a boss joins. Bosses alternate two skills, enrage at half HP and always drop a rare item.
  11. Items: drag onto a hero (1 each). Drop the same item on it, or two of the same on each other, to enchant it (+1, +2). 📜 Unbinding Scroll takes a hero's item off.
  12. Samurai hit harder below half HP (Bushido). Frost Healers heal over time and can wrap an ally in Ice Ward: it can't act, heals, ignores physical damage but takes 20% more magic damage.
  13. Occultists curse enemies: armor break (+25% damage taken), mana drain, slows, and Dispel strips shields and buffs.
  14. Upgrade the shop for one more field slot and better odds of pricier heroes. Lock keeps the shop for one round (rerolling releases it).
  15. Sell items by dragging them onto the shop or right-clicking: 2 gold (rare 4, scroll 1), times the enchant level.
  16. Bosses reward a choice of 3 blessings that last the whole run.
  17. Statuses: 💫 Stun 1s · 🤐 Silence 2s · 🌫️ Blind 2s · 🚫 Disarm 1s · 🐌 Slow · ☠️ Poison · 🧊 Frozen — ice skills may freeze; any direct hit shatters it. Bosses can't be frozen.
  18. Shop odds: 2💰 heroes from round 4, 3💰 from round 8, 4💰 from round 12. "Lock" keeps the shop for next round.
  19. Field slots: start with 3, buy more up to 9 (6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36 gold).
  20. Gold: 5 per round, +1 every 5 rounds (9 from round 20) + 1 interest per 10 saved (max 5) + a streak bonus for 2/4/6 wins or losses in a row (+1/+2/+3). Losing costs HP; at 0 the run ends.

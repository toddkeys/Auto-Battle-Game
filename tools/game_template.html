How to play
  1. Buy heroes in the shop. They wait on the bench (the wooden deck).
  2. Drag a hero to move it (drop on another hero to swap). Click selects; click empty ground to cancel. Right-click a hero or drop it on the shop to sell.
  3. 3 copies merge into ★★ automatically (3 × ★★ = ★★★).
  4. Bonuses: 2 or 3 different heroes of the same class or origin unlock a bonus (copies count once).
  5. Press Start battle — heroes fight on their own. A full blue mana bar casts their skill.
  6. Crusaders link to the nearest back-line ally (gold tether) and take part of its damage.
  7. Click a fighter during battle to see its live stats and skill.
  8. Selling returns half the gold — or all of it if you bought the hero this round.
  9. Summoners call allies (wisps, wolves, a stone guardian, a star drake) that fight until the battle ends.
  10. Every 5th round is a monster round; from round 15 a boss joins — six bosses, in a different order each run (the top bar names the next one). Bosses alternate two skills, enrage at half HP and always drop a rare item, even if you lose the fight.
  12. Items: drag onto a hero (1 each). Drop the same item on it, or two of the same on each other, to enchant it (+1, +2). 📜 Unbinding Scroll takes a hero's item off.
  13. Shields: gold shields stop physical damage, violet (🔮) magic shields stop magic damage.
  14. Gamblers roll dice and draw cards: random, sometimes huge, outcomes. Ice Wall (Glacier Warden) stops enemy projectiles until it breaks.
  15. Samurai hit harder below half HP (Bushido). Frost Healers heal over time and raise Ice Walls: enemy projectiles that fly into a wall hit it instead, and when a wall breaks or melts it shatters, damaging and slowing enemies next to it. The 2💰 Glacier Warden raises one, the 3💰 Frost Oracle one in front of the ally in danger (plus a shield), the 4💰 Winter Queen one great wall (twice as tough, plus a shield) that can Freeze enemies.
  16. Occultists curse enemies: armor break (+25% damage taken), mana drain, slows, and Dispel strips shields and buffs.
  17. When every blessing reaches its cap, boss rewards offer 3 gold, 2 player HP, or 2 free shop rerolls instead. At full HP, recovery becomes 1 gold + 1 free reroll. Choose one or skip; free rolls expire when battle starts.
  18. Upgrade the shop for one more field slot and better odds of pricier heroes. Lock keeps the shop for one round (rerolling releases it).
  19. Flank attacks: hitting a hero or monster from the side (above or below it) deals 10% more damage (yellow numbers), from behind (the side it isn't facing) 20% more (orange numbers). Assassins who leap behind the enemy and Grapplers who drag enemies past them make good use of it.
  20. Right-click an item to 🎲 reroll it into another random item of the same rarity, keeping its enchant level (30 gold), or to sell it. Sell items by dragging them onto the shop or right-clicking: 5 gold per item (an enchanted item counts every item that went into it). During battle you can still combine items in your bag.
  21. ⟲ Mimics steal enemy spells. The 4💰 Masked Magus copies the skill of a random living enemy — bosses too, a different one each time when it can — — the victim takes 220% damage and is silenced — and casts it with 25% more damage. The 2💰 Mirror Mage can only copy enemies that cost 2 gold or less (no bosses), but can copy fallen ones; with nothing to copy it blasts the area and inflicts a random debuff. Class bonus: mimics gain mana whenever an enemy casts.
  22. 🌀 Mesmers turn enemies on each other. Mesmerized enemies must attack one of their own allies. The 2💰 Puppeteer mesmerizes the strongest enemy for 2.5s; the 4💰 Rime Dragoness freezes the enemy standing among the most foes and makes every enemy near it attack it for 2s. Class bonus: mesmerized enemies take more damage.
  23. 🐒 Monkeys count their normal hits. Every 4th hit: the 2💰 Wukong attacks 30% faster for 3s; the 4💰 Mythic Wukong gains Sage's Fury — his next 4 hits deal +40% damage and he heals for 30% of all damage he deals (skills included) until they're used. Mythic Wukong's skill Ruyi Smash slams his staff in a long line: 160% damage that always crits and a 1s stun. Class bonus: the team dodges attacks.
  24. The 4💰 Gearmaster builds Arc Towers instead of turrets: their shots chain lightning to 2 more enemies. It starts every battle with one built, and its skill Storm Foundry builds more and supercharges them.
  25. 🔫 Gunslingers: the 2💰 Gunslinger fans 4 quick shots that can each crit; the 4💰 Deadshot snipes the farthest enemy with a piercing bullet. Their class bonus makes the whole team's critical hits hit harder. The 4💰 Gearmaster is the top Engineer: it builds two turrets at once and overclocks them.
  26. 🪝 Grapplers hook the enemy's back line and drag it in: the 2💰 Harpooner hooks the farthest enemy, the 4💰 Anchor Brute hooks the two farthest. Hooked enemies are stunned for 1s, and with the class bonus they take more damage for 3s. Bosses can't be dragged.
  27. ⚭ Fusion (from round 15, 100 gold): drag a ★★★ hero onto a ★★★ hero of another class. They become one hero that counts as both classes and both origins, has the best stats of the two (+10%), casts both skills at once (for 85% of their mana together) and can hold 2 items (both heroes keep theirs). Selling it returns both heroes' value.
  28. Bosses reward a choice of 3 blessings that last the whole run. Picking the same blessing again stacks it — the card and the blessing's tooltip show your total.
  29. Stars upgrade each class in its own way (healers heal more, summoners get stronger summons, druids root longer…) — the hero card shows what the next star brings. Items enchant up to +5; from +3 they gain a sub-stat. Support items: 🪔 Holy Censer, ⛲ Mana Fountain, 🔔 Warding Bell, 🚩 War Banner.
  30. Right-click a hero in the shop to mark it or remove its mark. Mark heroes you want in the 📖 Codex (☆, up to 10): they glow gold in the shop. Click any hero in the Codex or the Team Plan to see its full details.
  31. Runs (☰): Daily Challenge gives everyone the same shops, enemies and events for the day; Hard unlocks at round 15 and adds a run modifier. Every 4th round brings an event: a merchant, a mystery chest or a training ground.
  32. Ninjas dash between enemies. Gravity mages pull enemies together (Event Horizon holds them in a black hole). Druids 🌿 root enemies (rooted: can't walk or use moving skills, only attacks what is in range) and carry allies to safety. Berserkers are giants that spin their axes through crowds. Gambler cards fly as projectiles; summons' attacks carry a small elemental effect (burn, bleed, slow, mana drain…).
  33. Bards sing to nearby allies, one song every 4s: ♪ Haste (attack speed) → Guard (less damage taken) → Spirit (mana). The 1💰 Piper speeds up one ally (Rousing Note); the 4💰 Minstrel plays all three at once (Crescendo). Alchemists throw potions and earn gold. Necromancers raise fallen enemies to fight for you. Engineers build turrets. Monks dodge and get faster with every hit. Teleporters send melee allies onto the enemy carry (its strongest back-line hero) with a shield: the 2💰 Teleporter blinks in with the nearest one and strikes the carry, the 4💰 Warp Master sends the 3 nearest through a rift that stuns where they land.
  34. Sentinels stay near a ranged ally and react to the first nearby dive. Abjurers weaken spells and absorb burst. Duelists focus the strongest foe; Soulbinders share damage across linked enemies. Moonlance fires a straight arrow: longer travel means a longer stun, but enemies and Ice Walls can intercept it.
  35. Statuses: 💫 Stun 1s · 🎯 Taunt (must attack the taunter; hits on it are reflected back) · 🤐 Silence 2s · 🌫️ Blind 2s · 🚫 Disarm 1s · 🐌 Slow · ☠️ Poison: damage once per second, stacks up to 5, lasts at least 5s and cuts healing on the target by 30% (good vs healers) · 🔥 Burn: fire skills, damage twice per second, refreshes instead of stacking, deals double damage to shields and jumps to a nearby enemy when the target dies (good vs shields and groups). Fire melts Frozen; freezing puts out fire. A hero can be poisoned and burning at once · 🧊 Frozen — ice skills may freeze; any direct hit shatters it. Bosses can't be frozen.
  36. Shop odds: 2💰 heroes from round 4, 3💰 from round 8, 4💰 from round 12. Each shop Upgrade improves the odds; a fully upgraded shop late in the run rolls 1💰 heroes only 10% of the time, and each slot has a 5% extra chance to show a hero you already own (not yet ★★★). "Lock" keeps the shop for next round.
  37. Field slots: start with 3, buy more up to 9 (6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36 gold).
  38. Gold: 5 per round, +1 every 5 rounds (9 from round 20) + 1 interest per 10 saved (max 5) + a streak bonus for 2/4/6 wins or losses in a row (+1/+2/+3). Losing costs HP; at 0 the run ends.
